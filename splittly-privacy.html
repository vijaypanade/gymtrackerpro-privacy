Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Overview

Splittly does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything you enter — trips, expenses, names, amounts — lives only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No account or login required
  • No names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • No location or GPS data
  • No contacts access
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reports sent to any server

Local Storage Only

All data (trips, expenses, history, settings) is stored locally on your device using your phone's built-in storage. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data. There is no cloud backup, no sync, and no way for us to access your data.

Share Feature

When you share a trip summary or Trip Awards recap, your device's built-in share sheet is used. Splittly does not see, log, or store what you share or where you send it.

Third-Party Services

Splittly has no backend server, no database, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app works fully offline.

Children's Privacy

Splittly does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Since we collect no data, changes would only reflect new features, not new data practices.
